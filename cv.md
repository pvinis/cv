## What I'm brought in for

### React Native & Expo upgrades

I created and maintain [**upgrade-helper**](https://react-native-community.github.io/upgrade-helper),
the React Native core team's endorsed upgrade path, and [**rn-diff-purge**](https://github.com/react-native-community/rn-diff-purge),
the diff generator behind it. ‹fill: monthly users or company count, if you have the analytics›

If your app is stuck several versions back, this is the problem I've solved more times than
almost anyone. I take it as a fixed-fee engagement: an audit that tells you the real cost and
risk before you commit, then the upgrade itself.

### Native modules, when JavaScript runs out

Objective-C and Swift when the bridge isn't enough. At mycujoo I built the native modules for
live video streaming in both directions, the kind of work that decides whether a React Native
app can ship a feature at all.

### Mobile platform and developer experience

CI/CD, release pipelines, design systems, dev-tools. The work that makes a mobile team faster
every week instead of once. I led this at Artsy across every app the company shipped.

### Fractional mobile lead

Team of engineers, no one senior enough to own the architecture. I've led mobile teams at four
companies and can hold that seat part-time while you hire.
